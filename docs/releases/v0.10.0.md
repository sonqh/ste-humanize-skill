# v0.10.0: messages to users and customers

## For users

- The skill writes a reply to a user who is not technical, and turns an internal note into a message for a customer. It works in English and in Vietnamese.
- It decides if the reader is calm, confused, or upset. A chat reply has 4 sentences at most. A reply names the problem once, then gives the answer and the next step.
- It adds no promise or date that your note does not give. When the customer needs a date that you did not give, it names it on a `Missing:` line.
- A refusal starts with the no.
- In Vietnamese, it keeps one pair of pronouns, and it removes the stock phrases of support text.

## Install or update

