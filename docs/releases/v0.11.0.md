# v0.11.0: linter rules for messages

## For users

- The linter finds the stock phrases of support text, vague dates such as "soon" or "sắp tới", and phrases that blame the reader. It works in English and in Vietnamese.
- It flags a negative contraction such as "can't", and more than one "dạ" or "ạ" in a Vietnamese message.
- These five rules are advisory. They do not fail a run.
- In Vietnamese, the linter counts a sentence by syllables, with a cap of 35. Before, it counted each syllable as a word, with the cap of 25.
- A Vietnamese contrast with a claim that nobody made is now a hard finding, the same as its English form.

## Install or update

```
/plugin update ste-humanize@sonqh
```

The update applies after a restart of Claude Code.
