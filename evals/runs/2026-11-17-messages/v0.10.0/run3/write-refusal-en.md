Subject: Offline mode for the mobile app

Hi Tom,

Thanks for the kind words about the app. We will not build an offline mode in 2027. Handling sync conflicts costs too much to build.

Two things can help your drivers today:

- Draft save. When the network drops, the delivery form saves a draft on the phone and sends it when the signal returns. This works only for a form the driver opened before the signal was lost. It has been on since app version 4.2.
- Route sheet. You can print the route sheet of the day from the web dashboard, under Routes > Print.
