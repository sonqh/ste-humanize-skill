Subject: Offline mode for the mobile app

Hi Tom,

We will not build an offline mode for the mobile app. We have no plan for it in 2027, because handling sync conflicts costs too much for us to build.

Two things exist today that may help your drivers:

- Draft save. When the network drops, the delivery form saves a draft on the phone and sends it when the signal returns. It works only for a form that the driver opened before the signal was lost. Draft save has been on since app version 4.2.
- Route sheet. You can print the route sheet of the day from the web dashboard: Routes > Print.

Drivers who open the delivery form while they still have signal can finish it in the countryside.
