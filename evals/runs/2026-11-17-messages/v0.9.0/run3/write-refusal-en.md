Hi Tom,

Thank you for the kind words about the app. We will not build an offline mode in 2027. Handling the data conflicts that offline use creates costs too much to build, so we chose to put our work elsewhere.

Two things exist today that may help your drivers.

- Draft save. When the phone loses signal, the delivery form saves a draft on the phone. It sends the draft when the signal returns. This works only for a form that the driver opened before the signal was lost. It has been on since app version 4.2.
- Printed route sheet. You can print the route sheet of the day from the web dashboard. Go to Routes, then Print.

In areas with no signal, drivers should open the delivery form before they lose signal. They should also carry the printed sheet as a back-up.
