Hi Tom,

Thank you for the kind words about the app. We will not add an offline mode to the mobile app in 2027. Handling sync conflicts costs too much for us to build.

Two things can help your drivers today:

- Draft save. When the network drops, the delivery form saves a draft on the phone. It sends the draft when the signal returns. This works only for a form that the driver opened before the signal was lost. Draft save has been on since app version 4.2.
- Printed route sheet. You can print the route sheet of the day from the web dashboard (Routes > Print).

For countryside routes, drivers should open the delivery form before they lose signal.
