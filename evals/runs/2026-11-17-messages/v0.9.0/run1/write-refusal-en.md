Hi Tom,

Thank you for the kind words about the app. We will not build an offline mode in 2027. Handling sync conflicts costs too much to build.

Two things can help your drivers today:

- Draft save. When the phone loses signal, the delivery form saves a draft on the phone. It sends the draft when the signal returns. This works only for a form that the driver opened before the signal was lost. It is on since app version 4.2.
- Printed route sheet. You can print the route sheet of the day from the web dashboard. Go to Routes, then Print.

For a driver in the countryside, the best habit is to open the delivery form while there is still signal.
